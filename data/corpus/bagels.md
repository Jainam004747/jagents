# Bagels

Bagels are ring-shaped breads that are boiled briefly before baking. Boiling sets the crust and gives the bagel its chewy, glossy exterior. Montreal-style bagels are smaller and sweeter, boiled in honey water and baked in wood-fired ovens; New York-style bagels are larger, puffier and often boiled with malt.

Popular toppings include sesame, poppy seed and everything seasoning. A toasted bagel with cream cheese is a common breakfast in Canadian coffee shops.
