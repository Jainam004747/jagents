# Decaffeinated coffee

Decaf coffee has had most of its caffeine removed before roasting. Common methods are the Swiss Water Process, which uses water and activated carbon filters, and solvent methods using ethyl acetate or methylene chloride, which are removed again before the beans are dried.

Decaffeinated coffee still contains a small amount of caffeine, usually 2 to 5 milligrams per cup, compared with about 95 milligrams in regular brewed coffee. Many people choose decaf in the evening to avoid trouble sleeping.
