# Donuts

A donut is a fried dough confection. Yeast-raised donuts are light and airy because the dough rises before frying, while cake donuts use baking powder and have a denser crumb. Classic varieties include glazed, chocolate dip, Boston cream and jelly-filled.

Small round pieces of fried dough, sold as donut holes, became popular in Canada under the brand name Timbits. Donuts are best eaten fresh because they stale quickly once the oil cools and the starch begins to harden.
