# Espresso

Espresso is coffee brewed by forcing hot water through finely ground coffee under pressure, typically around 9 bars. A single shot uses about 7 to 9 grams of coffee and takes roughly 25 to 30 seconds to extract. The result is a small, concentrated drink topped with crema, a layer of fine foam made of oils and carbon dioxide.

Grind size matters more than anything else: too coarse and the shot runs fast and tastes sour; too fine and it runs slowly and tastes bitter. Baristas adjust the grind every day because humidity changes how coffee behaves. Espresso is the base for the latte, cappuccino, flat white and americano.
