# Coffee roasting

Green coffee beans are roasted to develop flavour. As beans heat up they dry, turn yellow, then brown through the Maillard reaction and caramelisation. Around 196 degrees Celsius the beans crack audibly, called first crack, which marks the start of a light roast.

Light roasts keep more of the origin's fruity and floral acidity, while dark roasts taste roasty, bitter and chocolatey. Roasted coffee is best brewed within a few weeks, after it has rested a few days to release carbon dioxide.
