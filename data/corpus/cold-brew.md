# Cold brew

Cold brew coffee is made by steeping coarse grounds in cold or room-temperature water for 12 to 24 hours, then filtering. Without heat, fewer acids and bitter compounds dissolve, so cold brew tastes smooth, sweet and low in acidity.

It is usually brewed as a concentrate at a ratio of about 1 to 5 and diluted with water or milk before serving. Cold brew keeps in the refrigerator for up to two weeks. It is different from iced coffee, which is hot-brewed coffee poured over ice.
