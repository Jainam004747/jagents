# Black, green and oolong tea

All true tea comes from the same plant, Camellia sinensis. The difference is processing. Green tea is heated soon after picking to stop oxidation, so it stays green and grassy. Black tea is fully oxidized, giving dark leaves and malty flavours. Oolong is partially oxidized and sits between the two.

Brewing temperature matters: green tea prefers water around 75 to 80 degrees Celsius, while black tea is brewed with water near boiling. Steeping too long releases tannins and makes tea bitter. Herbal infusions such as peppermint or chamomile are not technically tea at all.
