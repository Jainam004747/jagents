# Pour-over coffee

Pour-over brewing means pouring hot water by hand over coffee grounds in a paper filter, as with a Hario V60 or a Chemex. The paper filter traps oils and fines, so the cup is clean and bright, and the flavours of light roasts come through clearly.

Technique: rinse the paper filter, add medium-fine grounds, and pour a small amount of water first to let the coffee bloom for 30 to 45 seconds while carbon dioxide escapes. Then pour slowly in circles. Total brew time is usually three to four minutes. A gooseneck kettle gives better control over the flow of water.
