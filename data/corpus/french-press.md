# French press brewing

The French press is an immersion brewer: coarse coffee grounds steep directly in hot water for about four minutes, and then a metal mesh plunger separates the grounds from the liquid. Because the metal filter lets oils and fine particles through, French press coffee has a heavy body and a rich texture.

A common ratio is 1 gram of coffee to 15 grams of water. Use water just off the boil, around 93 to 96 degrees Celsius. Stir after one minute, press slowly, and pour immediately; coffee left sitting on the grounds keeps extracting and becomes bitter.
