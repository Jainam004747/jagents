# Greater Sudbury

Greater Sudbury is a city in Northern Ontario, Canada, known for nickel mining and its many lakes. The Sudbury Basin, one of the largest known impact structures on Earth, formed when a meteorite struck the area nearly two billion years ago, and it holds rich deposits of nickel and copper.

Landmarks include the Big Nickel, a nine-metre replica of a Canadian five-cent coin at the Dynamic Earth science centre, and Science North, an interactive science museum on Ramsey Lake. Laurentian University is located in the city. Winters are long and cold, which makes hot coffee very popular.
