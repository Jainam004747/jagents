# Latte art

Latte art is the pattern made by pouring steamed milk into espresso. It requires well-textured milk, called microfoam, with tiny bubbles and a glossy surface, and a fresh shot of espresso with crema.

The barista starts pouring from higher up to let the milk sink below the crema, then lowers the pitcher close to the surface so the foam floats and forms a shape. The most common designs are the heart, the tulip and the rosetta. Whole milk is easiest to work with because its fat and protein create stable foam.
